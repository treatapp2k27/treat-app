## Brand & Style

This design system expresses joy, social warmth, and spontaneous indulgence. Crafted for urban food lovers, budget-conscious diners, and culinary tastemakers, the identity balances culinary excitement with functional clarity. The emotional atmosphere is appetizing, effortless, and celebratory.

The visual direction combines modern minimalism with warm, tactile card surfaces and rounded interactive elements. Generous negative space balances saturated coral highlights and golden amber badges. Typography feels clean yet human, pairing friendly modern sans-serif geometric headlines with crisp, legible body text. Surfaces evoke fresh ceramic plating through luminous off-white backdrops, featherlight warm shadows, and smooth pill contours.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from culinary craft, fresh ingredients, and celebratory dining rituals:

- **Primary (`#FF5A36`)**: Ripe Persimmon Coral. Used for core brand gestures, primary CTAs, active tab accents, key price highlights, and heart/favorite reactions.
- **Secondary (`#FFAE1A`)**: Warm Apricot Amber. Reserved for social streak counters, rating stars, budget progress highlights, limited-time perk badges, and celebratory moments.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Crisp Cilantro Emerald. Utilized for positive discount tags, savings indicators, verified community baker/chef badges, and open-now operational status.
- **Neutral (`#211D1C`)**: Deep Charcoal Roast. Serves as high-contrast primary typography and icons, preventing the stark sterility of pure black while anchoring warm vibrant accents.

### Surface Tones & Containers
- **Canvas Base**: `#FBF9F7` (Porcelain Warmth) keeps visual fatigue low across photo-heavy feeds.
- **Surface Elevation 1**: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Pure White) for feed cards, sheet modals, and input fields.
- **Surface Elevation 2**: `#F3EFEA` (Stone Cream) for subtle divider rules, chip backgrounds, and recessed map cards.
- **Muted Text**: `#78706D` (Warm Pewter) ensures accessible AA body support, timestamps, and dietary micro-copy.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans serves as the primary typographic voice across all roles. Its geometric construction, generous counters, and gentle terminals provide a friendly and approachable feel while remaining legible at small scales in fast-paced ordering flows.

- **Headlines & Titles**: Display and headline styles use heavier weights (700 and 800) with tight negative letter-spacing to build personality and rhythm on restaurant editorial pages and discovery feeds.
- **Body Text**: Tuned for effortless readability when scanning reviews, ingredient callouts, and dietary menus.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Medium and bold weights with subtle positive tracking enhance readability for quick-glance information like tags, delivery times, deal counters, and pill navigation controls.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an 8-point spatial system (with a 4px sub-grid for icons and badges) to produce balanced mobile-first compositions.

### Form Factors & Grids
- **Mobile (<640px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `screen-margin-mobile` (16px) margins and 12px gutters. Feed cards span full width, while community story bubbles and horizontal deal carousels bleed cleanly off the screen edge using 16px inset clipping.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px)**: 8-column fluid layout with `screen-margin-tablet` (24px) margins and 16px gutters. Deal feeds and social curation boards reflow into a balanced 2-column card matrix.
- **Desktop (>1024px)**: 12-column layout constrained to a maximum content container of 1180px with centered auto-margins, supporting side-by-side interactive split views (e.g., discover map on the left, community review stream on the right).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth combines warm-tinted ambient shadows with distinct layered surface planes, avoiding harsh neutral blacks:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: `#FBF9F7` background tone. No shadow. Used for base screens and non-interactive sections.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Tiles)**: `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(66, 36, 26, 0.05)`. Applied to deal tiles, social feed post wrappers, and category pills in rest states.
- **Level 2 (Floating Modals & Active Cards)**: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(66, 36, 26, 0.08)`. Applied to sticky cart counters, drop-down sorting trays, and active deal carousels during drag interactions.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Sheet Drawers)**: `0px 16px 40px -8px rgba(66, 36, 26, 0.14)`. Applied to bottom action sheets, community review composers, and restaurant checkout panels.
- **Low-Contrast Separation**: When cards sit over complex food photography, an inner frosted glass backing (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) combined with an ultra-thin 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` provides instant legibility.

## Shapes

The shape system centers on balanced rounded geometries (Level 2: 0.5rem base, 1rem `rounded-lg`, and 1.5rem `rounded-xl`), contrasted with full circular/pill silhouettes on key interactive targets:

- **Cards & Visual Containers**: Apply `rounded-xl` (24px) to primary feed items, restaurant imagery thumbnails, and deal cards to reinforce a soft, tactile presence.
- **Modals & Bottom Sheets**: Top corners adopt `rounded-xl` (24px) or 28px for smooth bottom-sheet docking.
- **Buttons, Tags & Input Fields**: Use full pill radii (`9999px`) to create signature touchpoints that feel friendly and easy to tap on mobile screens.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill**: Full pill shape (`rounded-full`), background `#FF5A36`, label in white `#FFFFFF` (`label-lg`). Padding is 14px vertical by 24px horizontal. On press, scales to `0.98` with an amber-tinted shadow lift.
- **Secondary Pill**: Pale peach background (`#FFF0EC`), text `#FF5A36`, no border. Provides subtle visual emphasis for actions like "Save to List" or "View Menu".
- **Floating Action Pill**: Floating checkout or review bar, combining an amber deal highlight (`#FFAE1A`) on the left with a primary coral trigger on the right.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Filter Chip**: Compact pill shape (`rounded-full`), 8px vertical by 16px horizontal. Inactive state uses white fill with a 1px border in `#F3EFEA` and text in `#78706D`. Active state features a coral fill (`#FF5A36`) with crisp white text.
- **Budget Badge**: Pill with subtle warm amber background (`rgba(255, 174, 26, 0.15)`), text in `#B37400`, accompanied by a coin or treat icon.

### Cards
- **Deal & Spot Card**: Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-xl` (24px) corner radius, Level 1 shadow. Image sits flush to top edge with a 16:10 aspect ratio and rounded top corners (20px). Bottom content zone includes a 16px padded area showcasing the dish/venue title, tertiary green savings tag, distance indicator, and heart bookmark button.
- **Community Review Card**: Padded 16px container featuring circular user avatar (40px), bold username, timestamp, dish photo strip with 12px rounded thumbnails, and an interactive "Craving this" pill button with counter.

### Input Fields
- Fully rounded pill inputs (`9999px`) with an inner background of `#FFFFFF`, 1px border in `#E8E2DC`, 16px horizontal padding, and search icons tinted in `#78706D`. Focus states transition smoothly to a 1.5px `#FF5A36` border with a subtle 3px glowing ring in `rgba(255, 90, 54, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Radio**: 22px circle. Selected state shows a solid `#FF5A36` background containing a centered 8px white circle. Unselected shows a 1.5px border in `#D4CCC6`.
- **Dietary Filter Checkbox**: 22px box with 7px rounded corners. Selected state is solid `#FF5A36` displaying a crisp white checkmark icon.

### Social & Community Elements
- **Treat Streak Indicator**: Pill badge featuring an active flame icon, secondary amber fill (`#FFAE1A`), white bold text, and a slight tilt (`-1.5deg`) to create playful social momentum.
- **Split-the-Bill Progress Bar**: 8px height track with `rounded-full` edges in `#F3EFEA`, filled dynamically with a primary-to-secondary warm gradient (`#FFAE1A` to `#FF5A36`).
